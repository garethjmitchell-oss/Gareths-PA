# Tool 8: Kickstart

## Purpose
Overcomes the emotional barrier to starting a task — the "Wall of Awful." Addresses the feeling that prevents task initiation, not just the task size. This is what's missing from Magic ToDo: breaking a task into 8 steps doesn't help if the problem is that every step still feels impossible.

## Why It Matters for ADHD
The Wall of Awful is built from layers of past negative experiences with a task type (shame from procrastinating, fear of failure, boredom, overwhelm). It's an emotional barrier, not a logical one. You can't logic your way through it — you need to lower the emotional temperature first, then use a dopamine primer to get the brain's reward system engaged, then enter through the smallest possible door.

## Triggers
`8`, `kickstart`, `wall`, `can't start`, `avoiding`

## Process
1. Ask: "What's the task, and what does avoiding it feel like?" (skip if already stated)
2. Name the barrier type and respond accordingly:
   - **Boring/pointless** → pair with a reward, make it a game (timer challenge, playlist, points)
   - **Overwhelming** → shrink to the absolute minimum viable version of the task
   - **Fear/shame** → acknowledge directly: "This feels loaded. That's normal. The task isn't judging you."
   - **Unclear** → clarify what "done" actually looks like before starting
3. Suggest a dopamine primer matched to the barrier
4. Give ONE micro-entry action — something so small it requires almost no willpower
5. Say: "Do the primer, then do just that one thing. Tell me when it's done."

## Dopamine Primer Examples (by barrier type)
- **Boring** → favourite playlist, podcast, TV show in background, snack
- **Overwhelming** → 5-min walk, cold water, change of location
- **Fear/shame** → write down one thing you've succeeded at recently, then start
- **Unclear** → write a one-sentence definition of "done" first

## Micro-Entry Examples
- For a phone call: "Open your contacts and find the number. That's it — don't call yet."
- For a difficult email: "Open a new email and type just the subject line."
- For a form: "Find the form and open it. Just look at page 1."
- For cleaning: "Pick up one object and put it in the right place."

## Detail Levels
| Level | Behaviour |
|-------|-----------|
| Less detail | Skip barrier naming → primer + micro-action only |
| Default | Full process: name barrier, primer, micro-action |
| More detail | Explore emotional history of why this task feels hard; longer-term strategies |

## Example

**Input:** `8 I have to call my landlord about the boiler and I keep putting it off`

**Output:**

This one has some weight to it — probably a combination of **not wanting to deal with conflict** and the discomfort of making an unplanned call.

**Primer:** Put on a song you like. Make a cup of tea. Sit somewhere comfortable.

**Micro-entry:** Open your phone contacts and find your landlord's number. Don't call yet — just find it.

Tell me when that's done and I'll walk you through the next step.

*Tell me when that first step is done.*
