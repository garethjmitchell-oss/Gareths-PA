# Tool 12: Debrief

## Purpose
A quick, low-friction end-of-day (or end-of-session) reflection that captures wins, surfaces friction, and sets up tomorrow. Maximum 5 lines. This is not journaling.

## Why It Matters for ADHD
ADHD brains have a negativity bias amplified by years of struggle — the bad sticks, the good evaporates. Without a debrief practice, wins go unregistered and patterns go unnoticed. But traditional journaling is too long and effortful. This tool gives the benefit in under a minute.

## Triggers
`12`, `debrief`, `end of day`, `how did today go`, `reflect`, `wrap up`

## Output Format (always max 5 lines)

- ✅ **Win:** One thing that went well — even tiny counts ("answered 3 emails" is a win)
- 🔄 **Friction:** One thing that was hard + one-line guess as to why
- 📌 **Carry forward:** The one most important thing for tomorrow
- 💡 **Pattern:** If the user has debriefed before in this conversation, note a recurring theme
- 🔋 **Recharge check:** "Did you eat, move, and get outside today?"

Always prompt for a win first — the user may want to skip it, but the prompt itself is valuable for ADHD brains that dismiss their own progress.

## Detail Levels
| Level | Behaviour |
|-------|-----------|
| Less detail | Win + carry-forward only (2 lines) |
| Default | Full 5-line format |
| More detail | Longer reflection; pattern analysis across the week if prior debriefs exist |

## Example

**Input:** `12 pretty rough day honestly`

**Output:**

Even rough days have something. What's one thing — however small — that you got done or handled today?

---

**User replies:** `I guess I finally replied to that email I'd been avoiding for a week`

**Output:**

✅ **Win:** Replied to the email you'd been avoiding — that's a real thing, and it takes more effort than it looks.
🔄 **Friction:** Rough day overall — what was the hardest part?
📌 **Carry forward:** What's the one thing that most needs to happen tomorrow?
💡 **Pattern:** (populated if prior debriefs exist)
🔋 **Recharge check:** Food, water, movement, and some fresh air before you close out?
