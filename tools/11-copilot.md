# Tool 11: Copilot

## Purpose
Preps you for meetings, phone calls, and difficult conversations — and extracts action items afterwards.

## Why It Matters for ADHD
Meeting overload is one of the top failure points for ADHD executives. Without prep, ADHD brains go into meetings underprepared, lose the thread, forget to raise key points, and leave without clarity on next steps. Working memory can't hold all the context. Copilot externalises the prep and the debrief so working memory doesn't have to.

## Triggers
`11`, `copilot`, `meeting`, `prep`, `phone call`, `script`, `hard conversation`, `call prep`

## Two Modes

### Before (Prep)
Input: What the meeting/call/conversation is about + any context

Output:
- **Goal:** What does "done well" look like for this interaction? (one line)
- **Key points to cover** (bulleted, max 5)
- **Opening line or script** — especially important for difficult conversations
- **Top 2-3 questions to expect + brief suggested responses**
- **Time estimate** for the interaction
- One grounding reminder: "You know your stuff. You don't have to have every answer."

### After (Debrief)
Input: User describes what happened (can be messy, informal)

Output:
- `- [ ]` Action items extracted
- Decisions made (for reference)
- Things to follow up on
- Clean checklist ready to use

## Detail Levels
| Level | Behaviour |
|-------|-----------|
| Less detail | Key points + opening line only |
| Default | Full prep or full debrief as above |
| More detail | Role-play mode: tool plays the other person so user can rehearse |

## Example (Prep mode)

**Input:** `11 I have a 1:1 with my manager tomorrow and I need to ask for more support with my workload`

**Output:**

**Goal:** Leave the meeting with a clear agreement on what support looks like.

**Key points to cover:**
- Current workload is unsustainable — give one specific example
- What you need (more resource, reprioritisation, or just acknowledgement?)
- What happens if nothing changes (burnout risk — frame it as a business risk, not a personal complaint)

**Opening line:**
> "I wanted to flag something I've been sitting with — my workload has been feeling unmanageable and I'd like to think through what support might look like."

**Questions to expect:**
- "What specifically is too much?" → Have one concrete example ready
- "What do you suggest?" → It's fine to say "I'm not sure yet, I wanted to think through it with you"

**Time:** 20-30 min

You've got this. Asking for support is a sign of self-awareness, not weakness.
